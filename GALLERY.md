# Screenshot gallery

These screenshots show the standalone Memory Desk showcase with each bundled
theme. The Spaceship HUD palettes share a design, so `spaceship-red-dark`
represents the 20 color and accent variants. `spaceship-2` is shown separately.

Aurora Glass and the two Leather Style previews are full-page captures at
1440 CSS pixels wide and 2x pixel density, so their material details can be
inspected at higher resolution.
